# Direct Tax Companion — Income-tax Act, 1961 → 2025

A section-wise comparison of the **Income-tax Act, 1961** and the **Income-tax Act, 2025** (Act No. 30 of 2025), built as a quick-reference aid for practising professionals and CA students.

Prepared by **SAAKSH And Associates LLP**, Chartered Accountants.

**Live:** https://sachhinholmukhe.github.io/SAAKSH/

---

## Why this exists

The Income-tax Act, 2025 came into force on **1 April 2026**, repealing the 1961 Act after 65 years. Tax policy did not change — rates, slabs, thresholds and reliefs carry over. What changed is the architecture:

| | 1961 Act | 2025 Act |
|---|---|---|
| Sections | 819 | 536 |
| Chapters | 47 | 23 |
| Schedules | 14 | 16 |
| Year concept | Previous Year + Assessment Year | Tax Year |
| Change in rates | — | None |

Almost every section number you know by heart has moved. This tool maps them.

## What's inside

- **202 section mappings**, covering every chapter — basics, exemptions, salary, house property, business and profession, presumptive taxation, capital gains, other sources, clubbing, aggregation, losses, deductions, rebates and relief, the new regime, returns and assessment, TDS/TCS, interest and penalties, and transition.
- **Colour-coded change badges** on every row, so you can see *how* a provision moved, not just where: `Same No.` · `Renumbered` · `Relocated` · `Merged` · `Split` · `Concept change` · `New`.
- **Sec. 393 payment codes** — a searchable table of 35 TDS/TCS codes with rate, threshold, sub-clause slot and the legacy section each replaces, plus the new form numbers (138, 140, 143, 144, 131, 168).
- **Live search** that accepts a section number (`44`, `80C`), an old TDS section (`194J`), a payment code (`1027`) or a plain keyword (`rent`, `depreciation`, `crypto`).
- **Study mode** — blurs the 2025 Act numbers so students can self-test, tap a row to reveal.
- **Starred sections**, category filters, and a light/dark theme.
- **Transition panel** setting out what moves to the 2025 Act and what stays under the 1961 Act.
- **First-quarter practice checklist** and an FAQ for common client and student questions.

## Notable structural changes

1. **819 → 536 sections.** Provisos and explanations absorbed into the main text; tables and formulas replacing narrative prose; obsolete provisions dropped.
2. **One year concept.** "Previous Year" and "Assessment Year" are both gone, replaced by a single **Tax Year** (Sec. 3).
3. **TDS consolidated.** 40+ scattered provisions collapse into **Sec. 392** (salary), **Sec. 393** (all other payments, by payment code) and **Sec. 394** (TCS).
4. **Sec. 10 relocated.** Exempt incomes move out of the main body into **Sec. 11 read with Schedules II–VII**.

## ⚠️ Verify before you cite

**These mappings are a working reference, not an authority.**

Section numbers of the 2025 Act here are compiled from a published chapter-wise concordance and cross-checked against the bare text of the Act wherever possible. Be aware that several secondary mapping tables in public circulation **contradict one another** — some place Sec. 80C at 100 and salary TDS at 170, which does not reconcile with the Act's own internal cross-references. Rows tagged **Verify** in the tool are the least corroborated.

Payment codes tagged **Provisional** await final CBDT confirmation, and the code list covers common payment types rather than the full 1001–1092 range.

Before quoting any section number or payment code in a **return, challan, TDS statement, notice reply, contract, opinion or advice**, confirm it against:

- the bare Income-tax Act, 2025, and
- the official CBDT section-mapping utility on [incometax.gov.in](https://www.incometax.gov.in)

## Two compliance traps worth repeating

- **Sec. 206AA survives** (no valid PAN) as a rate overlay — deduct at the higher of the code rate, 20%, or 5% for the goods-purchase, e-commerce and immovable-property codes.
- **Secs. 206AB / 206CCA are abolished.** Any non-filer rate-doubling logic still running in accounting or ERP software is over-deducting, and will need refund adjustments to vendors.

## Which Act applies

| Situation | Governed by |
|---|---|
| Income earned up to 31 March 2026 (FY 2025-26 returns) | **1961 Act** — even though filed after April 2026 |
| Income earned from 1 April 2026 (Tax Year 2026-27) | **2025 Act** |
| TDS/TCS on payments made on or after 1 April 2026 | **2025 Act** — new section codes on challans |
| Pending assessments, appeals and proceedings for pre-April-2026 periods | **1961 Act** — saved by Sec. 536 |
